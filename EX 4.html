Training Glossary

Progressive Overload
The gradual increase of stress placed upon the body during exercise training.
RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion)
A scale from 1 to 10 used to measure workout intensity based on repetitions in reserve.

Trainer's Daily Pro Tip

"Consistency always outperforms intensity. Focus on executing proper movement mechanics with full range of motion before increasing resistance."
