About FitPulse

Physical fitness is not merely about lifting weights; it is a holistic lifestyle that cultivates discipline, cardiovascular resilience, mental clarity, and enduring strength. At FitPulse Training Club, we offer evidence-based coaching for both beginners starting their fitness journey and elite athletes aiming for peak performance.

Key Fitness Terminology

Hypertrophy
The enlargement of muscular tissue cells resulting from targeted resistance training.
Repetition Maximum (1RM)
The maximum amount of weight a person can lift for a single repetition of a given exercise.
Caloric Deficit
A nutritional state where fewer calories are consumed than expended, leading to fat loss.

Did You Know?

Drinking adequate water during intense workouts boosts muscle endurance by up to 15%. Muscles are composed of roughly 75% water, and even mild dehydration impairs power output and recovery rate!
